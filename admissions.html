How to apply

  1. Choose your admissions route. SRMIST publishes separate guidance for India and international applicants.
  2. Check your programme and campus. The programme listings cover fees, eligibility, and selection criteria. Check the applicable entrance or counselling instructions there.
  3. Follow the programme’s application instructions. Confirm dates, document requirements, and the current form before submitting.
