Summary

Every page now scores 93 or higher on mobile speed and 100 on accessibility, best practices and SEO in Lighthouse. Pages download 26–76% less, depending on the page. On the 19 text samples checked over photos and the 3D scene, none now falls below the contrast minimum; two did before. All 51 visitor-flow checks pass on the production build, including live Gemini answers.

Read the home score with care. The landing intro still keeps the page hidden for up to 6.9 seconds on a first visit, unless the visitor skips it. Lighthouse appears to count the intro artwork as the main content, so 98 overstates how soon a first-time visitor reaches the page. Its removal or shortening is a design decision, listed under next steps.

What these results don't cover

  • Scores come from a software-rendered test browser on one PC and a local server, using emulated device sizes. Real phones, slower 3D hardware and Vercel will differ.
  • Lighthouse ran just before two final small changes: the 3D start fix and the footer link height. Neither adds weight, but the scores weren't re-measured after them.
  • No screen reader or disabled user has tried the site. Automated tools catch only part of accessibility problems.
  • Real microphone input and speech output weren't tested.
  • Contrast over the 3D scene changes as the view moves. It was sampled at the starting view.
  • Sign language isn't provided.

Recommended next steps

  • Before presenting: replace the Gemini key, set GEMINI_API_KEY on Vercel, and try the deployed site on a real phone.
  • Decide on the intro: shorten it, or show it only once with the page visible behind it. This is the largest remaining wait, and it's a design call for your team.
  • Test with people: run NVDA and a phone screen reader through chat, search and the 3D section.
  • Replace sample content: the 3D panel still shows sample fees and degrees, marked as samples. Confirm or remove them before any public launch.
  • Clean up: the old journey animation and school dialog can no longer be opened after the merged navigation changes, and the repo holds about 9 MB of unused PNG originals. Remove them if your team agrees. They aren't shipped to visitors, and the PNGs are the sources for image regeneration.
  • Finish meaning-based search: run npm run index:embed across two days of the free tier. Keyword search works until then.
